# 竜の街 — the shared vocabulary

Every scene in this town is painted separately, so the only thing that makes
the same houses come out as the same houses is using the same words for them.
Paste these phrases; do not paraphrase them.

| thing | phrase |
|---|---|
| houses | three-storey timber-framed houses, dark oak framing on pale lime plaster, upper storeys jettied out over the street |
| roofs | steep dark blue-grey slate roofs, moss along the eaves, stone chimneys |
| paving | worn grey granite cobbles with a shallow drainage channel down the middle |
| the square | a cobbled square about forty metres across |
| the fountain | a carved grey stone fountain, a coiled dragon spouting into a round basin |
| the inn | the Golden Dragon: a building half again as large as its neighbours, a gate arch through to an inner courtyard, a carved and painted dragon sign hanging from an iron bracket |
| market goods | cloth awnings in faded red and ochre over wooden trestles, baskets, barrels, sacks |
| the river | fast clear green water about twenty metres wide, running over pale stones |
| the bridge | a single-span arched bridge of pale grey stone with low parapets and iron lantern posts |
| the ridge | steep wooded slopes of dark broadleaf trees rising to the north |
| the back lanes | washing lines strung between upper storeys, firewood stacked against plaster walls, water butts |
| the far bank | a smaller cluster of the same slate roofs on the south bank, and a cart track leaving south between hedgerows |

Rules, learned the hard way:

1. **Read the bearings off the massing render**, `worlds/dragon/raw/blockouts/<node>.png`,
   not out of your head. It is in the same projection as the output.
2. **Name what lies between things.** Water never meets a road along a bare
   line: say what the bank is.
3. **Nothing ends within sight.** A street leaves the frame or turns a corner;
   it does not stop.
4. **Do not mention the sun, the time of day or the weather.** The sun
   paragraph is generated from the survey and appended for you.
5. **Do not write style into a scene.** The town's style lives in world.json.
6. Never name a studio or an artist.
